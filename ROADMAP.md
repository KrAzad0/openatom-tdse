# Roadmap

Development goals, not commitments or evidence of existing adoption.

- Validate initial Coulomb and 1D propagation solvers; establish domain and time-step convergence.
- Add reproducible analytic benchmarks and documented pulse units.
- Add controlled absorber and flux methods; validate against reference calculations.
- Extend to angular momentum coupled channels, then investigate toroidal confinement with center-of-mass and internal coordinates.
- Publish a versioned release after tests and review; invite independent users and contributors; consider PyPI and archival DOI when mature.
