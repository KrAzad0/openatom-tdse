# Roadmap

Development goals and status; not claims of established adoption.

1. Radial Coulomb and 1D TDSE foundation: initial development pre-release.
2. Two-state solid-torus composite atom: implemented with numerical tests.
3. Six-state bound hydrogen model: implemented, subject to review and CI.
4. MATLAB comparison: CSV comparison and matched-parameter workflow provided; independent numerical agreement pending.
5. Pulse parameter studies: amplitude sweep provided; research-scale parameter survey pending.
6. Convergence: grid and time refinement tools and finite-matrix tests; research-scale convergence pending.
7. Visualization: reproducible pulse and population plots from computed CSV.
8. Software-paper draft and archiving: working draft and protocol; manuscript review, release archive and DOI pending.

Future: controlled absorbing boundaries and continuum methods; additional magnetic sublevels; physical field modeling; independent user feedback and package publication after review.
